# Landing page spec (user's request, condensed; quoted copy is exact and must be used verbatim)

Role: world-class UI/UX, senior React, CRO, edtech product design. Build a stunning, professional, responsive landing page for the AI curriculum-aligned platform for Ghanaian students and teachers. Use Coursera (coursera.org) as a reference for quality, navigation, hierarchy, resource discovery and search, but do NOT copy its branding, logos, graphics, exact layouts or text. Original identity. Inspect the project first; integrate without breaking auth, the student dashboard, admin or routes. No static mockup: every button, link, search and CTA must work.

## 2 Identity
Value proposition: "Learn smarter. Understand better. Achieve more."
The platform turns official learner manuals into notes, AI illustrations, step-by-step explanations, exercises and assessments, games and revision, a subject AI tutor, personalized recommendations, and progress analytics. Students register and choose level, class, programme (Learning Area) and subjects, then get a personal space containing only their authorized subjects. Emphasize understanding, competency, practical application and active learning over rote learning. Product name and logo must be configurable in one place. Working name "LearnSmart AI" unless the repo already defines an official name (the platform settings have an app name/branding; check them and use the configured value).

## 3 Tech
Existing stack: React+Vite+TS, Tailwind, TanStack Query, React Router, Lucide, existing components/auth/APIs. Use the existing design system or create reusable tokens. No unnecessary libraries. Use real backend data; where none exists, use clearly separated mock services. Never fabricate user counts, partnerships, testimonials, ratings or outcomes.

## 4 Design
Premium international edtech feel, accessible and Ghana-relevant. Clean editorial layout, white space, clear type hierarchy, deep navy for trust, teal/emerald accents, warm restrained interactive accents, high-quality illustrations and authentic imagery, subtle micro-interactions, rounded cards with restrained shadows. Avoid heavy gradients, glassmorphism, clutter, animated backgrounds, generic AI text, giant heroes that push content below the fold, and fake stats or endorsements. Premium on desktop, excellent on mobile.

## 5 Sections in order
A Announcement bar: slim, dismissible, admin-configurable or disableable. Example: "Your curriculum. Your learning journey. Powered by AI."
B Nav: logo and name on the left. Menu: Explore Subjects, How It Works, AI Tutor, Practice & Games, For Teachers, For Schools. On the right: search icon or compact input, Log In, prominent "Start Learning Free". Accessible mobile menu. Explore Subjects goes to the discovery section or a subjects page. How It Works goes to that section. AI Tutor goes to the tutor intro or demo. Practice & Games goes to that section. For Teachers goes to a teacher info page, For Schools to an institutional page. Log In goes to the existing login, Start Learning Free to existing registration/onboarding. Create any missing routes; no dead links.
C Hero: two columns on desktop, stacked on mobile. Label "THE FUTURE OF LEARNING IS PERSONAL". H1 "Your Curriculum. Your AI Tutor. Your Path to Success." Paragraph "Turn your school subjects into an engaging learning experience with clear notes, interactive illustrations, personalized AI tutoring, practice questions and educational games—all aligned with your curriculum." Primary CTA "Start Learning Free", secondary "Explore Subjects". Under them: "Built around approved curriculum materials. Designed for active learning." (no claim of government endorsement). Right column: a polished product-preview composition (not stock) with a dashboard, subject cards (Mathematics, Integrated Science, English Language, Social Studies; configurable), an illustrated lesson preview, an AI tutor chat, a progress viz and a quiz preview. Clearly a preview; reuse real screens where possible. Subtle entrance and float animations; respect reduced motion.
D Search, directly under the hero: "What do you want to learn today?" / "Discover subjects, topics, lessons and practice activities." Placeholder "Search a subject, topic or concept...". Searches subjects, topics, published lesson titles, keywords and levels. Icon, clear button, empty states. Popular searches are configurable (Algebra, Photosynthesis, Chemical bonding, Essay writing, Fractions, Environmental protection). Results respect publication status and permissions; anonymous users see public previews only. Debounced, real backend API (add a public search endpoint if needed).
E Browse subjects: "Explore your subjects" / "Find the topics you study in school and explore a better way to understand them." Subject cards with icon or illustration, name, short description, level, published-lesson count only when real, and an "Explore Subject" action. Filters (admin-configurable): JHS, SHS, Languages, Mathematics and Sciences, Humanities, Business, Computing and Technology. Selecting one shows public published resources, or goes to registration for personalized access. Never show inaccessible content as available.
F How it works: "A smarter way to learn, step by step". 1 Choose your subjects - "Tell us your school level, programme and the subjects you study." 2 Explore your learning materials - "Access structured notes and illustrations generated from approved curriculum resources." 3 Ask your AI tutor - "Get explanations, examples and guided help when a concept is difficult." 4 Practise and improve - "Strengthen your understanding through quizzes, interactive games and personalized revision." Numbered or connected cards, stacked on mobile.
G AI tutor: "Meet your personal AI learning companion" / "Ask questions in your own words. Explore difficult concepts. Get explanations grounded in your learning materials." A safe simulated exchange. Student: "Can you explain photosynthesis in simple terms?" Tutor: "Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants use sunlight, water and carbon dioxide to make food. Let's break it down step by step." Then a small diagram, short explanation, suggested practice question, and a source reference labelled illustrative unless real. CTA "Explore AI-Powered Learning" goes to the real tutor route (authenticated) or to registration. Never pretend the demo came from a real manual.
H Lessons: "Go beyond reading. See how ideas work." Explain that manuals become structured lessons. Grid: illustrated explanations, step-by-step examples, concept maps, labelled diagrams, real-life scenarios, practical activities, summaries. A realistic lesson-preview card with subject, topic, objectives, illustration, key explanation and a short knowledge check. CTA "Explore Interactive Lessons". Public content must be sample or approved preview only.
I Practice: "Make every minute of practice count" / "Test your understanding, learn from mistakes and build confidence with interactive activities designed around what you study." Cards for quick quizzes, match-the-concept, flashcards, diagram labelling, timed challenges, topic revision and problem solving. Show explanatory feedback. A working sample quiz (select, feedback with why, reset), labelled as a demo unless real. CTA "Start Practising".
J Personalized: "Your subjects. Your goals. Your learning space." / "Every student follows a different learning journey. Choose your subjects and access the materials relevant to your studies." Preview with selected subjects, recommended next lessons, recent topics, practice, progress and revision recommendations. Explain that authorized subject selections drive access and the backend enforces it securely. CTA "Create My Learning Space" goes to registration and subject selection.
K Teachers: "Give every lesson a stronger foundation". Explore curriculum resources, prepare materials, assign practice, review results, identify misconceptions, recommend remedial resources, track class progress where authorized. Classroom or teacher-dashboard visual. CTA "Explore Teacher Tools". Only claim implemented features; mark planned ones or omit them.
L Schools: "Bring smarter learning to your school". Centralized curriculum resources, school and class management, teacher access to approved materials, student analytics, configurable review workflows, controlled subject/resource access. CTA "Discover Learning for Schools", going to an institutional enquiry page or contact form if onboarding isn't self-service. No invented partnerships or testimonials.
M Why choose: grid of curriculum-grounded content, personalized subject access, AI explanations, illustrated notes, interactive practice, games, progress tracking and flexible access. Concise, benefit-focused, with icons.
N Testimonials only if genuine approved ones exist. Otherwise "Designed around the way students learn", showing educational principles, example workflows and approved demo material. Never invent names, photos, quotes, results or endorsements.
O FAQ accordion (accessible), with answers that reflect REAL capabilities and policies: 1 What is this learning platform? 2 Which educational levels does it support? 3 How are the learning notes generated? 4 Does the AI tutor use approved learner manuals? 5 Can I select only the subjects I study? 6 How do the quizzes and games work? 7 Can teachers use the platform? 8 Can schools manage their students? 9 Can I access lessons on my phone? 10 Can I use learning materials offline? 11 Is the platform free? 12 How does the platform protect student information? Don't promise free access, offline use, formal approval or privacy beyond what's implemented.
P Final CTA: "Your next breakthrough starts with understanding." / "Choose your subjects, explore your learning materials and take the next step in your learning journey." Buttons "Start Learning Free" and "Explore Subjects".
Q Footer: logo and short description; Explore Subjects, How It Works, AI Tutor, For Students, For Teachers, For Schools, Help Centre, Contact, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Accessibility, copyright. Only links that exist or have defined destinations. Legal text configurable (it already exists in settings).

## 6 Auth integration
Anonymous visitors: explore public sections, search public subjects and previews, read, register, log in. Signed-in students: "Start Learning" and the logo go to their dashboard; show a contextual "Continue Learning". Teachers: a clear path to the teacher workspace. Admins: a link to the admin dashboard when authorized. Never show the admin link to students. Server enforces everything.

## 7 Responsive
Mobile-first. Desktop, laptop, tablet, phone. Compact mobile nav, reachable primary actions, natural stacking, no horizontal overflow, touch-friendly search, readable type, optimized images, usable cards and accordions, reduced motion.

## 8 Perf/SEO
Semantic HTML, heading hierarchy, title and meta description, Open Graph, alt text, lazy below-fold images, responsive images, code splitting, efficient rendering, optimized assets, focus indicators, keyboard nav, reduced motion, contrast. No heavy assets up front, no broken or placeholder images. No structured-data claims about accreditation, ratings or organizations.

## 9 Interactions
Restrained: nav hover, search suggestions, card hover, smooth anchors, accordion transitions, quiz feedback, button loading states, subtle hero animations, scroll reveals where helpful. Touch-friendly, reduced motion, never block content. Don't animate everything.

## 10 Content management
Make the content admin-configurable where practical, through the Admin Panel (System Settings / a new Landing page section): product name and logo, hero copy, announcement bar, CTA labels and destinations, featured subjects, popular searches, featured lessons, feature descriptions, teacher/school sections, FAQs, footer links, public announcements. Structured config model, validated before publish. If a full CMS isn't feasible, build a clean config layer and document the hookup.

## 11 Components
AnnouncementBar, Navbar, MobileNavigation, HeroSection, LearningSearch, PopularSearches, SubjectExplorer, SubjectCard, HowItWorks, AITutorShowcase, IllustratedLessonPreview, PracticeAndGames, PersonalizedLearningShowcase, TeacherFeatures, SchoolFeatures, FeatureGrid, TestimonialsOrLearningPrinciples, FAQAccordion, FinalCTA, Footer. Modular and testable, TS interfaces, UI kept separate from API and logic.

## 12 Tests
All nav links work; login/register CTAs; search returns valid results; subject cards go to the right place; FAQ accordion; sample quiz; authenticated nav; public visitors can't reach restricted materials; mobile/tablet/desktop; no inert buttons; no broken images or console errors; axe on major interactions; acceptable performance on slow connections; existing student and admin routes still work.

## 13-15
Process: inspect, reuse, tokens, nav+hero, search+discovery, showcases, teacher/school, FAQ/CTA/footer, wire routes/APIs, responsive/a11y/SEO, test, report. Real code, not a static design. Where the backend lacks something, state the limitation and provide a clean integration point; never fake success. Acceptance: polished and professional at the level of leading international edtech, Ghana-tailored, communicates tutor/notes/practice/games, discovery works, auth integrated, teacher/school offerings clear, search and interactions work, no fabricated claims, responsive, accessible, performant, existing functionality preserved.
