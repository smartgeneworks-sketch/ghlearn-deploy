## Approval

Your school administrator or the GH Learn team reviews your application. If it is approved, you get an email titled **Your GH Learn account is ready** (and a text message, if you gave a number). If it is declined, the email gives the reason.

## Set your password

1. Open the button in the email. The link works once and expires after **72 hours**.
2. On **Set your password**, choose a new password (10+ characters with a letter and a number) and confirm it.
3. Press **Save password and sign in**.

## If the link does not work

- If it has expired or was already used, you will see **This link no longer works**. Press **Request a new link**, or ask your school administrator to use **Resend invite**.
- If you already chose a password, just [sign in](/login). If you forgot it, use [Forgot password](/forgot-password).
