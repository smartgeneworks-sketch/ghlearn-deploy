---
title: Review teacher applications
audience: school_admins
order: 10
summary: Approve or reject teachers who applied to join your school.
---

1. Open **Admin panel**, then **Users and Permissions**, then the **Teacher applications** tab. A red number shows how many are waiting.
2. Open an application under **Waiting for review**. Check the name, contact details, school, subjects and forms, and any staff ID or licence number.
3. To approve: choose the **Subjects to assign** and press **Approve**. The teacher account is created and the teacher gets an email (and an SMS if they gave a number) with a link to set their password.
4. To reject: enter a **Reason for rejecting (sent to the applicant)** and press **Reject**.

If the teacher did not receive the email or the link expired, open the application and press **Resend invite**. A new link replaces the old one.

> If the applicant typed a school that is not on GH Learn yet, a super admin must create the school (or match it to an existing one) before the application can be approved.
