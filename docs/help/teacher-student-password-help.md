---
title: Help a student who cannot sign in
audience: teachers
order: 50
summary: Give a one-time reset code or a temporary password.
---

First, suggest that the student uses [Forgot password](/forgot-password) with their email or verified phone number.

If that does not work:

1. Open **Teaching**, open the class and then the student.
2. Under **Password help**, choose one option:
   - **Create reset link**: you get a link and a 12-character code, shown once and valid for 24 hours. The student opens the link, or enters the code at **Enter your reset code**, and chooses a new password. Their current password keeps working until then.
   - **Set temporary password**: a temporary password is shown once. Their current password stops working and they must choose a new one when they next sign in.
3. Give the code or temporary password to the student in person. Do not send it in a group chat.

Both actions are recorded in the audit log and the student is told in the app. You can only do this for students in your own classes.
