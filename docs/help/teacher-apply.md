## Steps

1. Open [Apply for a teacher account](/register/teacher). You can also use **Sign up** at the top of any page and choose **Teacher application**.
2. Enter your full name, email and, optionally, your mobile number.
3. Choose your region and your school. If it is not in the list, tick **My school is not listed** and type the school name.
4. Choose the subjects and forms you teach. You can add your staff ID, teacher licence (NTC) number and a note for the reviewer. These are optional but help the reviewer.
5. Press **Send application**.

You see **Application received**, and we email you (and text you, if you gave a number) to confirm.

> Do not create a student account to use as a teacher. Teacher accounts are created only after approval.
