---
title: Manage classes and enrolments
audience: school_admins
order: 30
summary: Create classes, add teachers and enrol students.
---

1. Open **Admin panel**, then **Classes and Enrolments**.
2. Press **Add class**. Enter the class name, year of study and academic year. For a subject class choose the subject; leave it empty for a form class.
3. Open the class. Under **Teachers**, use **Add a teacher**. Under **Find students to enrol**, search and select students, then enrol them.

## Good to know

- The subject of a class cannot change while students are enrolled.
- **Archive class** hides it from teachers and lists. Students stay enrolled and records are kept, and you can restore it.
- At the end of the year, use **Change academic year** to plan and apply moving classes to the next year.
- **Export enrolments** downloads a list of who is in which class.
