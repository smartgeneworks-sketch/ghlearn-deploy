## Start a practice session

1. Go to **My subjects**, open a subject and choose **Practice & tests**.
2. Under **Start a practice session**, choose the topic, mode, number of questions and, if you like, a time limit in minutes.
3. Press **Start**, answer the questions and press **Submit answers**.

You see your score straight away. Some written answers get a provisional mark until your teacher confirms it.

## Work set by your teacher

Work from your teacher is listed under **Set by your teacher**. If it has a time limit and time runs out, your saved answers are submitted automatically. Correct answers may only be shown after the assessment closes.

## See how you are doing

Your past attempts are under **History**, and **My progress** shows your progress across subjects.
