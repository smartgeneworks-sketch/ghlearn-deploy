## Open the tutor

1. Go to **My subjects** and open a subject.
2. Press **Ask the tutor**.

## Choose a mode

- **Hint first**: the tutor guides you step by step instead of giving the answer straight away. Best for homework.
- **Explain**: a full explanation of a topic or idea.
- **Check my answer**: paste your working or answer and the tutor checks it.

Type your question under **Your question** and press **Send**. Use **New conversation** to start a fresh topic. You can mark answers as helpful or not helpful.

> The AI tutor can make mistakes. Check important facts with your teacher or your learner manual. Never share passwords, your phone number or your home address in a chat.

There is a daily limit on messages so the tutor stays available for everyone. Chats are deleted automatically after 180 days.
