## Publish your availability

1. Open **Meetings**.
2. Under **My availability**, choose the start time and duration, and whether the session is **In person** or **Online** (or both).
3. For in-person sessions add the room or location. For online sessions add the meeting link (it must start with https://), for example a Google Meet or Zoom link.
4. Press **Add slot**.

Students at your school can then request your open times, or propose another time.

## Answer requests

Requests appear in **Meetings** and in your notifications. You can **Accept**, **Decline**, or **Reschedule** by proposing a new time; the student must accept the new time. If you add an online link when accepting, the student sees **Join online meeting**.

> Sessions happen on the outside video service. GH Learn does not host or record them. Booked slots cannot be removed; cancel the booking first.
