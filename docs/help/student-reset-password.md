## By email or SMS

1. On the [sign-in page](/login), choose **Forgot password**, or open [Forgot your password?](/forgot-password).
2. Enter your email, or your phone number if you have verified it.
3. By email: open the link we send and choose a new password. By SMS: press **Enter the code**, type the code from the text message and choose a new password.

## With help from your teacher or school

If you cannot get the email or SMS, ask your teacher or school administrator. They can give you a one-time reset code or a temporary password.

- **Reset code**: open [Enter your reset code](/reset-password), type the code and choose a new password.
- **Temporary password**: sign in with it. You will be asked to choose a new password straight away.

> Never share your password. GH Learn staff will never ask for it.
