---
title: Reset a password or unlock an account
audience: school_admins
order: 50
summary: Help any user at your school who cannot sign in.
---

1. Open **Admin panel**, then **Users and Permissions**, and open the user.
2. Under **Security and recovery**, choose:
   - **Create reset link**: a link and a 12-character code, shown once and valid for 24 hours. Their current password keeps working until they use it.
   - **Set temporary password**: shown once. Their current password stops working, all sessions sign out, and they must choose a new password at next sign-in.
   - **Unlock account**: clears the lockout after repeated failed sign-ins.
   - **Sign out everywhere**: ends all sessions without changing the password.
3. Give codes or temporary passwords in person.

Every action is recorded in the audit log, and the user is told in the app. Students can also reset their own password by email or verified phone at [Forgot password](/forgot-password).
