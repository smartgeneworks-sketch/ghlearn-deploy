## What you need

- an email address you can open
- a password with at least 10 characters, including a letter and a number
- optionally, a Ghana mobile number (recommended, so you can sign in and reset your password with your phone)

## Steps

1. Open [Sign up](/register). You can also use **Sign up** at the top of any page and choose **Student account**.
2. Enter your full name, email and password. Add your mobile number if you have one.
3. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian to agree first. You can add their email under **Parent/guardian email (optional)**.
4. Tick the box to agree to the terms of use and privacy notice, then create your account.
5. On **Set up your learning space**, choose your region, type your school name, then choose your level, year of study, learning area (optional) and subjects. Press **Next**, then **Confirm**.

> Type your school name the way your school writes it. If it matches a school on GH Learn, you can see your school's teachers and book sessions with them.

You can change your subjects later from **My profile**.
