## Create a class

1. Open **Teaching**.
2. Under **New class**, enter a class name (for example 1 Science A), choose the subject and year, and press **Create class**.

You can only create classes for subjects you are assigned to. If you see **You have no assigned subjects yet**, ask your school administrator.

## Add students

1. Open the class and go to **Members**.
2. Under **Add students**, tick the students and press the **Add** button (it shows how many you picked).

The list shows students at your school who take that subject (and year). Students appear only after they have signed up and their school name matches your school. If a student is missing, ask them to check the school name in **My profile**.

## Work with the class

From the class you can **Set new work for this class**, see **Mastery by topic**, **Weak topics** and **Repeated mistakes**, mark written answers under **To mark**, set remedial practice, and download a **PDF report**. Use **Archive class** at the end of the year; nothing is deleted.
