## Before you start

You can only book teachers and counselors at your own school. Your school name in **My profile** must match a school on GH Learn. If it does not, you will see a message asking you to check the spelling or ask your school to join.

## Steps

1. Open **Meetings**.
2. Under **Request a meeting**, choose the teacher or counselor.
3. Pick one of the **Available times**, or use **Or propose a time** if none suit you.
4. Enter the topic and, if you like, a note. Press **Send request**.

You get a notification when the teacher accepts, declines or suggests a new time. If they suggest a new time, press **Accept new time** to confirm it.

## Joining

For online sessions, press **Join online meeting** at the time of the session. The call happens on an outside service (for example Google Meet or Zoom), not inside GH Learn. You can **Cancel** a request if you can no longer attend.
