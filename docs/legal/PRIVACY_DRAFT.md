## 3. What we collect

**Account details:** your name, email address, a securely hashed password (we never see the password itself), your role (for example student or teacher), and optionally your date of birth and a parent or guardian's email.

**Phone number (optional):** if you add a Ghana phone number, we send a one-time code by SMS to verify it, so you can sign in with it and reset your password by SMS.

**School and studies:** your region, school name, level, year, learning area and subjects, and your learning preferences.

**Learning activity:** lessons you open, practice answers and scores, progress, rewards, saved lessons and classes you belong to.

**AI tutor chats:** the messages you send to the AI tutor and its replies.

**Meetings:** requests to meet a teacher, the topic, the chosen time, messages about the request and the external meeting link.

**Teacher applications:** the details applicants provide, such as name, contact details, school and subjects, and our decision.

**Enquiries:** what you send through the Contact or For Schools forms.

**Security and technical records:** sign-in times, IP address and browser details in security and audit logs, and records of messages we sent (for example that an SMS was delivered).

## 4. Why we use it

- to create and run your account and keep it secure (Act 843: performing our agreement with you, and our legitimate interest in security);
- to provide lessons, practice, progress tracking, the AI tutor and meetings;
- to let your teachers and your school's admins see the progress of students in their classes;
- to send messages you need: verification codes, password resets, account approvals and meeting notices;
- to understand how the service is used, in totals, so we can improve it;
- to meet legal obligations and deal with misuse.

We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell it.

## 6. Cookies and storage on your device

We use only cookies that are needed for GH Learn to work:

- `gl_at` and `gl_rt` keep you signed in (session cookies, up to 14 days);
- `gl_csrf` protects forms against cross-site attacks.

Your browser also stores your sidebar preference and a sign-out signal so all your open tabs sign out together. Lessons you save for offline use stay on your device. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, or third-party analytics.

## 8. Your rights

Under Act 843 you can ask us to:

- tell you what information we hold about you and give you a copy;
- correct information that is wrong;
- delete information, or stop or limit how we use it;
- explain any decision made about you.

Contact us using the details in section 1. We may need to check your identity first. A parent or guardian can make these requests for a child. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

## 9. Security

We protect information with encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, limits on sign-in attempts, automatic sign-out after inactivity, role-based access and audit logs. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

## 10. Changes

If we change this notice in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app or by email before the change takes effect.
