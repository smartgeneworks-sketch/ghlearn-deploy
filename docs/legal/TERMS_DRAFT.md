## 1. What GH Learn is

GH Learn is an online learning service for Senior High School students in Ghana and their teachers. It offers lessons, practice questions, an AI tutor, progress tracking and a way to book sessions with teachers.

Our lessons and questions are prepared using the official learner materials published for the Ghanaian curriculum and are **aligned to the NaCCA curriculum. GH Learn is not produced, approved or endorsed by NaCCA, the Ghana Education Service or the Ministry of Education.**
